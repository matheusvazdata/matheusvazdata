# 👋 Hi, I'm Matheus Vaz

I am an **Information Technology Analyst** (*Analista de Tecnologia da Informação*) and federal civil servant at the **Federal University of Maranhão (UFMA)**, Brazil.

I work at the **Artificial Intelligence Directorate** (*Diretoria de Inteligência Artificial — DAI*) of **AGETIC** (*Agência de Tecnologia da Informação*), UFMA's information technology agency. According to the [university's organizational chart](https://portalpadrao.ufma.br/sti/organograma), the directorate's mandate is to develop and apply AI tools to automate administrative processes, improve teaching and learning, and support research and outreach (*extensão*).

My background is in data engineering and analytics: pipelines, workflow orchestration, data modeling, and dashboards. I now bring that background to applied AI in the public sector, with reliable data, clean code, and reproducible pipelines.
